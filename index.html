How to use this tracker

Drag along the timeline to move between FOMC meetings. Hold still or drag slowly to zoom in to individual meetings, push toward either edge to keep moving, and flick quickly (or let go) to zoom back out. The line above the timeline is the federal funds rate target (the upper bound of the range since December 2008). Amber marks are unscheduled meetings. The selected statement is shown with changes compared to the previous meeting: green words were added and red strikethrough words were removed. Arrow keys step one meeting, Page Up/Down jump a year, and Enter toggles zoom.
